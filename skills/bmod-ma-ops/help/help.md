# ma-ops knowledge

This document covers the skills of the `ma-ops` module: what each one gives the user and when to recommend it. The module's agents (Alex, Yael, Amit and Gadi, group `ops`) are listed in `roster.toml`.

## Skills

- `ma-ops-dev-epic`: orchestrates one epic of the initiative store end to end, story by story, through `bmad-agent-po` and `bmad-build`. Recommend it when the user asks to run, develop or orchestrate a whole epic.
